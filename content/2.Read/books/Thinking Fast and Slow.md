desc: "NEW YORK TIMES BESTSELLER The guru to the gurus at last shares his knowledge with the rest of us. Nobel laureate Daniel Kahneman's seminal studies in behavioral psychology, behavioral economics, and happiness studies have influenced numerous other authors, including Steven Pinker and Malcolm Gladwell. In Thinking, Fast and Slow, Kahneman at last offers his own, first book for the general public. It is a lucid and enlightening summary of his life's work. It will change the way you think about thinking. Two systems drive the way we think and make choices, Kahneman explains: System One is fast, intuitive, and emotional; System Two is slower, more deliberative, and more logical. Examining how both systems function within the mind, Kahneman exposes the extraordinary capabilities as well as the biases of fast thinking and the pervasive influence of intuitive impressions on our thoughts and our choices. Engaging the reader in a lively conversation about how we think, he shows where we can trust our intuitions and how we can tap into the benefits of slow thinking, contrasting the two-system view of the mind with the standard model of the rational economic agent. Kahneman's singularly influential work has transformed cognitive psychology and launched the new fields of behavioral economics and happiness studies. In this path-breaking book, Kahneman shows how the mind works, and offers practical and enlightening insights into how choices are made in both our business and personal lives--and how we can guard against the mental glitches that often get us into trouble."

# 阅读笔记

## 一、先判断这本书值不值得读（5–10分钟）
快速扫一遍：
1. **目录**
2. **序言 / 前言**
3. **每章开头和结尾**
4. **随机翻几页**
回答三个问题：
- 这本书在讨论什么问题？
- 作者的核心观点是什么？
- 对我有什么用？
如果答案很模糊，就不需要精读

## 二、带问题阅读（最关键）
1 作者在回答/研究什么问题？
2 作者的核心观点是什么？
3 作者为什么这么认为？(用什么论证,证据是否可靠,适用范围)
4 对我有什么用？ (这个观点可以解释什么现实？,对我有什么帮助?)

## 三、只记录思想,不记录内容
**作者观点**
>如:社会制度往往是路径依赖的，一旦形成很难改变

**解释/论证**
>如:制度会产生既得利益群体，他们会阻止改变

**我的想法**(联系类似现实,对我有什么用)
>如:中国很多行业监管也是这种路径依赖
