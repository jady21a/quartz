## What I'm tinkering with

- **Building a personal blog** — turning my notes into a website for free with Obsidian + Quartz
- **Obsidian workflows** — practical setups I've stumbled onto while living in Obsidian
- **AI & automation** — letting tools handle the repetitive work so my productivity system actually runs itself
- **Reading & watching** — turning what I take in into something of my own, instead of forgetting it the moment I'm done

## On "why I built this site"

Going it alone, it's easy to lose to procrastination and forgetting. Putting the process out in the open forces me to actually understand what I'm doing, and hopefully helps fellow tinkerers along the way.
