## What I'm tinkering with

- **Building a personal blog** — turning notes into a website for free with Obsidian + Quartz
- **Obsidian workflows** — practical setups I've found along the way while using Obsidian
- **AI & automation** — letting tools handle the repetitive work so my productivity system actually runs
- **Reading & watching** — turning what I take in into something of my own, instead of forgetting it the moment I'm done


> [!tip] Whatever's copyable from my posts, I open-source it — just grab it and go.



## Why I built this site

Going it alone, it's easy to lose to procrastination and forgetting. Putting the whole process out in the open forces me to actually understand what I'm doing — and hopefully helps fellow tinkerers like you along the way.
